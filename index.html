iPhone Solo needs live gravity readings. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone, allow motion access, then face the screen toward the sky.

Allow Motion

Solo folds the picture as you roll your iPhone. It uses the motion sensors and nothing leaves your device.

Add to Home Screen

Solo is made to run as an app. From the Home Screen it opens full screen, without Safari’s bars, and keeps your photo.

  1. Tap Share in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add and open Solo from the icon.
