Allow Motion

Solo folds the picture as you roll your iPhone. It uses the motion sensors and nothing leaves your device.

Add to Home Screen

Solo is made to run as an app. From the Home Screen it opens full screen, without Safari’s bars, and keeps your photo.

  1. Tap Share in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add and open Solo from the icon.

Read the Lid

Solo folds the picture as you close your MacBook. Chrome reads the hinge angle straight from the lid sensor and nothing leaves your Mac.

Solo folds the picture as you close your MacBook, but this browser cannot talk to the lid sensor. Open the page in Chrome for the real hinge, or preview the fold with the trackpad.

  1. Click Allow lid sensor.
  2. Pick the Apple device in Chrome’s list and connect.
  3. Close the lid slowly.

Add to Dock

Solo opens in its own window, without browser bars. In Safari choose File → Add to Dock. In Chrome use Install from the address bar.
